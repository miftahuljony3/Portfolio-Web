Between "Pay" and "Paid" one payment, start to finish, in about 8 seconds

  1. 0.0s

    Customer taps Pay

    The hosted checkout opens on their phone.

  2. 0.4s

    Number allocated

    One of your wallet numbers is assigned to this payment alone.

  3. ~6s

    Money sent

    The customer pays in their own bKash or Nagad app.

  4. ~7s

    Receipt SMS arrives

    It is forwarded, signed and stored raw before parsing.

  5. ~8s

    Matched & paid

    Amount, number and TrxID match exactly one open payment.

  6. ~8s

    Webhook fires

    A signed payment.success event reaches your server.

Every feature 16 capabilities, all live today

  • Hosted checkout

    A phone-first payment page: pick a wallet, get a number, pay from the wallet app. Nothing to build.

  • Automatic confirmation

    The operator's receipt SMS is matched by amount, number and TrxID within seconds, with no manual approval.

  • One API, both directions

    The same four wallets take payments and send them, money in and out on one key.

  • Payouts in seconds

    POST /v1/payouts and an agent phone dials USSD itself. Live runs have completed in 12 and 15 seconds.

  • Payment links

    No website or developer needed. Share a link on Facebook, WhatsApp or SMS.

  • QR payments

    Every link carries a QR code for counters, posters or invoices, with no terminal required.

  • Signed webhooks

    HMAC-signed events, retried with backoff, with every delivery attempt visible in the portal.

  • Instant SMS notifications

    Account events such as go-live, reviews, paused webhooks and new teammates reach you by SMS.

  • Risk-scored payouts

    Large amounts, new payees, bursts and repeat numbers are scored, and high-risk payouts wait for a person.

  • No card data, no PCI scope

    No card rails means no card numbers ever enter the system, so there is nothing to leak.

  • Your money stays yours

    Paid straight into your own wallet numbers. No float held and no settlement cycle.

  • Merchant portal

    Payments, checkouts, links, API keys, webhook logs and team roles, built mobile-first.

  • 2FA & audit trail

    Two-factor sign-in, IP-locked revocable API keys and an append-only log of staff actions.

  • Public status page

    A status page anyone can read, plus a health endpoint your own monitoring can poll.

  • Sandbox from day one

    Test the whole integration, payouts included, before approval, then swap one key for live.

  • Human onboarding

    Invite-only and checked by a person, with minimal paperwork.

07 Engineering principles

Built to withstand production.

  1. 01

    Understand before acting

    Read the whole problem, pull out the explicit and implicit requirements, and gather context before touching code. No assumptions left unexamined.

  2. 02

    Weigh trade-offs first

    Break complex work into components and compare approaches before building. Choose the design that holds up, not the one that's quickest to type.

  3. 03

    Design for failure

    Anticipate failure modes and operational load: retries with backoff, idempotency, escalation paths, public status and health checks.

  4. 04

    Reuse over duplication

    Prefer shared abstractions and clear boundaries, so systems stay maintainable as teams and features grow.

  5. 05

    Secure by default

    Signed webhooks, two-factor auth, IP-locked keys, role-based access and append-only audit trails. Least privilege everywhere.

  6. 06

    Verify, don't assume

    Run it, test it and check it live. Report outcomes honestly, then make the small adjacent improvements that clearly add value.
